# Credits and asset boundaries

The accessory meshes, textile patterns, emblem geometry, documentation, viewer and styling helper in this pack were created for this conversation. They do not contain a downloaded base avatar, existing song audio, a copied celebrity outfit, or textures from the earlier concept images.

The new assets and scripts are supplied for the user's use and modification in this video project. No claim is made that these rights replace or broaden any third-party source-avatar license.

The eidoverse repository's credits identify `claude_suit.vrm` as modelled by **digi**, provided under **CC-BY**, and identify the Claude logo-bloom character design as by **voooooogel**. The returned credits do not specify a CC-BY version. Preserve the source metadata and verify the applicable terms before redistributing a modified base avatar. The base avatar is not bundled here.
